Common Physical Security Vulnerabilities
Before the assessment, review these key physical attack techniques that exploit the vulnerabilities you observed:
Tailgating
Following an authorized person through a secured entrance without using your own credentials. The attacker relies on social pressure — people often hold doors open out of politeness.
Piggybacking
Similar to tailgating, but with the authorized person's knowledge or consent. Often occurs when visitors claim to have forgotten their badge or are carrying heavy items.
Shoulder Surfing
Observing someone entering credentials, PINs, or sensitive information by looking over their shoulder. Can happen at keypads, computer terminals, or badge readers.
Dumpster Diving
Searching through trash or recycling bins for sensitive documents, discarded equipment, or information that wasn't properly destroyed before disposal.
Card Cloning
Copying the data from an access card using a portable reader. Attackers can then create duplicate cards to gain unauthorized access to secured areas.
